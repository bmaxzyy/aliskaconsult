    About Aliska Commodities

    We are a natural produce trading house working at origin. Our buyers live in the growing regions, our agronomists train the cooperatives, and every lot is graded, tested and traceable back to the farm group that grew it.

    • Direct trade with 60+ farmer cooperatives
    • Every lot lab-tested for purity and moisture
    • Full documentation: phytosanitary, certificate of origin, quality certs
    • Flexible terms — FOB, CIF or local delivery
