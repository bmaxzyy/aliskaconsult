How we measure success

Lives and livelihoods improved — not reports delivered

Every engagement targets at least one of two outcomes:

1

Less poverty

Evidence, policy and tools that put income and opportunity in more hands.

2

Growth that lasts

Formalisation, resilience and data-driven decisions that keep it going.
