Our practices

Three practices, one chain of work

Research tells us what is true, policy decides what should change, and digital products put that change into people’s hands. A fourth line, business and financial advisory, helps organisations act on it.

  1. 01

    Research & Insight

    What is really happening, and why.

  2. 02

    Policy & Development

    What should change — designed to be implemented, funded and measured.

  3. 03

    Digital Products

    Change, delivered into people’s hands at scale.

  4. +

    Business & Financial Advisory

    Helping organisations grow, restructure and comply as they act on it.

Practice 01

Research and Insight

We find out what is really happening, and why. Our field teams and analysts gather evidence where official data runs thin: in informal markets, rural communities and small businesses.

  • Socio-economic and poverty studies
  • MSME and informal-economy surveys
  • Market, sector and commodity research
  • Baseline, monitoring and impact evaluations
  • Data analysis, dashboards and indices
For
  • Government ministries and agencies
  • Development partners
  • Financial institutions and investors
Practice 02

Policy and National Development

We turn evidence into policies and programmes that can be implemented, funded and measured.

  • Policy design and regulatory impact assessment
  • National, sector and district development plans
  • Programme design for poverty reduction, jobs and inclusion
  • Tax and formalisation policy for MSMEs
  • Public-private partnership structuring and stakeholder engagement
  • Implementation planning, monitoring and evaluation frameworks
For
  • Ministries and regulators
  • Metropolitan and district assemblies
  • Development partners and industry associations
Practice 03

Digital Transformation Products

Good policy fails if it never reaches people. We design and build digital products that deliver it at scale.

Built by Trident Aliska Digital Tech

Sikaflow

A digital business management and financial formalisation platform for MSMEs. It works offline and on basic phones, and helps traders keep records, manage stock, become tax-ready and access credit.

  • Digital public infrastructure: digital identity, registries and payment rails
  • Digital transformation strategy and roadmaps for public and private institutions
  • Product design, piloting and scale-up with technology partners
For
  • Government, banks and fintechs
  • Telecoms and development partners
  • Enterprises
Practice 04

Business and Financial Advisory

For organisations that need to grow, restructure or comply.

  • Corporate strategy and business management
  • Financial advisory, corporate finance and risk management
  • Accounting and tax advisory
  • IT advisory
  • MSME development, formalisation and sustainability
  • Project management
For
  • Start-ups, MSMEs and mid-sized companies
  • Large companies and multinationals
  • Non-profits and individuals
