Who we are

Advisory Firm

Aliska Business Advisory and Research is an indigenous Ghanaian strategy and research firm, founded in 2011 and based in East Legon, Accra. We work with governments, regulators, development partners, businesses and communities to answer one question: what will actually reduce poverty and build lasting prosperity here?
