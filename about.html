Who we are

An indigenous Ghanaian strategy and research firm

Founded in 2011 and based in East Legon, Accra, Aliska works with governments, regulators, development partners, businesses and communities to answer one question: what will actually reduce poverty and build lasting prosperity here?

We answer it with evidence. Our researchers go where the economy really happens — into markets, farms and workshops — and our strategists turn what they find into policies, national programmes and digital products that people use. Through Trident Aliska Digital Tech, our joint venture with Nasdaq-listed Trident Digital Tech Holdings, we build the technology too.

How we work

From evidence to outcomes

Our method is simple and uncompromising: find what is true, decide what will work, and build it so it reaches the last mile.

  1. 01

    Research what is true

    We go into the markets, farms and workshops where Ghana’s economy actually happens, and gather evidence first-hand.

  2. 02

    Design what will work

    Our strategists turn the findings into policies, national programmes and plans built for the people they are meant to serve.

  3. 03

    Build the tools that deliver

    Through Trident Aliska Digital Tech we design and ship the digital products that carry these solutions to scale.

Purpose, vision & mission

Why Aliska exists

Purpose

To lift people out of poverty — sustainably

We use research to design the policies, plans and products that lift people out of poverty and keep growth sustainable.

Vision

An Africa where policy is grounded in evidence

A Ghana, and an Africa, where every policy is grounded in evidence and every citizen and business shares in the growth it creates.

Mission

Research, design, build, share

We research what is true, design what will work, build the tools that deliver it, and share the rewards with the people and partners who make it happen.

Our values

Built for the last mile

Our approach

From evidence to impact, and back again

Most firms stop at the report. We carry the work through to results, then use what we learn to make the next study sharper.

  1. 01

    Research

    We go to the market, the farm and the office to measure what is really happening.

  2. 02

    Policy

    We turn findings into policies, plans and programmes that are practical, fundable and measurable.

  3. 03

    Products

    Where delivery needs technology, we build it with our partners, so good policy reaches people at scale.

  4. 04

    Shared impact

    We track the results openly and share the rewards with our people, partners and the communities we serve. What we learn from real use feeds our next piece of research.

Leadership

Meet the Managing Director

Aleem Kumi

Managing Director & CEO, Trident Aliska Digital Tech

Aliska is an indigenous advisory firm that specialises in financial advisory and analysis services, research and data analysis, accounting services, strategy services, technological services and administrative paperwork support services.
Some of his published articles include “Why Facebook should pay tax in Ghana” and writing on fintech and credit markets.

As Managing Director of Aliska and CEO of Trident Aliska Digital Tech, Aleem leads the firm’s research, advisory and technology practice — from evidence gathering in the field to the digital products that deliver it.
